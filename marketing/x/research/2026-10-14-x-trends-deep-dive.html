X trends deep dive for @Heroes0fcrypto, week of 14 Oct 2026

Read-only research, pulled 14 Oct 2026 between 2:25 and 2:50 PM PT. Every number comes from X API public_metrics at fetch time. L = likes, RT = reposts, R = replies, V = views. Times are PT. I used about 30 X calls: 4 trend lists, 4 news searches, 14 post searches covering about 370 posts, and 2 profile lookups covering 37 accounts.

Method limits: - The search API does not support min_faves. I pulled 25 posts per query sorted by relevancy and then ranked them by likes myself. Treat this as a strong sample, not a full census. - The format and timing medians below come from this sample only.

3. Viral formats (what the winners share)

  1. Hook-as-structure. Emberville's "What / Who / Where / When" with a 39s clip. The post is scannable, carries one ask and one date, and won the week.
  2. Debate bait around AI. "AI games are just slop, meanwhile..." (7.4k views from 643 followers) and "you vibe coded a game..." (134 replies). Controversy plus a real artifact.
  3. A specific funny mechanic in a clip. Hypersect's cat cooks your food: one sentence, one 48s clip, a question at the end.
  4. Roadmap / plan images from tactics studios. MENACE roadmap: 256 likes on 8.3k followers, about 3% likes per follower, very high.
  5. Head-to-head comparisons. "Same prompt, two models" produces outsized views for tiny accounts (@keydol123, 116 followers, 7,746 views).
  6. Host threads. The hosts collect 50-100+ replies each week (#DemoMonday 102 replies, #WishlistWednesday 71, #TrailerTuesday 62, #ScreenshotSaturday 60-65). Visibility comes from replying, not from posting your own thread.
