## 3. Patterns
**What works**
1. Video or a real clip wins. The top post in the whole sample was a video (Hooded Horse, 18k views).
2. Community content gets more reach than the studio's own content. Lavapotion's fan-art repost got about 2.4x the views of its own posts.
3. Personality and team posts pull replies. 12 replies on the Lavapotion CM joke, the highest reply count from that account.
4. "If you like [known game], try this" works in the post itself, not only in the bio. Small accounts using it reach 600-1,000 views.
5. Weekly community threads (Turn Based Thursday, hosted by a different dev each Thursday) are where small tactics devs get 200-1,100 views from a few hundred followers. You join by replying to the host with a clip.
6. Concrete devlog detail ("text is now 10px+") beats generic hype for small accounts.

**What fails**
- Daily "find the best move" puzzle images with hashtags and CTAs get almost nothing. The ChessFlow series got 0 likes and 4-14 views per post, e.g. https://x.com/Chessflow_org/status/2074436615724744905. Puzzles only work with a real board, a real argument, and an answer posted later.
- Repeating the same "play free in browser" line across posts and replies plateaus at 2-7 likes (LitRPGforum).
- Hashtag stuffing and emoji CTAs ("Drop your move 👇") don't help in this sample.
- Token, raffle, and play2earn framing (the Olden Era name collision) attracts the wrong crowd.

**Timing and frequency.** The sample is too small for a confident best hour. Turn Based Thursday activity clusters on Thursdays (US morning to afternoon). The big accounts post about 1-3 times a day. Small devs who did well posted when they had something new, not on a fixed daily cadence. One post a day, as the calendar already plans, is fine.

## 4. Astra
**Most likely meaning:** "GPT Astra" / "GPT-6 Astra", an OpenAI model. X is full of "GPT Astra built this game" posts.
- Volume: 9,505 posts mentioning it from 7 Oct 1:26 PM PT to 14 Oct 1:26 PM PT (the 7 Oct figure is a partial day). The daily count rose to 1,822 on 13 Oct (UTC day).
- Top examples:
  - Roblox game built overnight: 146 likes / 21,658 views https://x.com/mihawkxxxxx/status/2096172309664973150 (54-follower account)
  - Rocket League clone for $25 in three.js: 35 likes / 6,211 views https://x.com/aimlapi/status/2096285113667424504
  - 3D model animation tips ("stop using Astra or Sol for 3D"): 405 likes / 31,953 views https://x.com/maxt3chno/status/2108166465505341539
  - "GPT-6 Astra played WoW without seeing the screen": https://x.com/OwenGregorian/status/2107102816045068382
- The pattern: tiny accounts get outsized reach with "AI built/played this" plus a real clip. The audience is AI builders, not tactics players.

**Uncertain:**
- I did not confirm Astra's official status or naming on an OpenAI page. Posts variously call it "GPT Astra", "GPT-6 Astra", and "Astra 6". Some claims in these posts (zero-days, a paused model, 10T parameters) are rumors.
- I don't know how HoC "uses" Astra. A19 is the production AI and per XCOM.md it is a bounded legal-move search. Astra might have been used to build or write the game, not inside it. Confirm with Stepan.
- Other Astras exist (Google's Project Astra, various crypto tokens named Astra). The October 2026 X volume points to the OpenAI model.

**How to ride it within the rules:**
- Only say what's true and checkable, e.g. "Parts of this game were built with GPT Astra. The opponent you fight, A19, is not an LLM: it searches legal moves on the real rules."
- Post one real board state from a real match (no fake clip), something like "A19 vs a person: who read the queue right?"
- Aim the Astra hook at the AI-builder crowd once or twice. Their attention converts poorly to tactics players, so it shouldn't become the account's identity.
- No invented stats. No "AI beats humans X%".

## 5. Recommendations for @Heroes0fcrypto
1. Fix the profile first: add the period, drop the 🟦, and rename to "Heroes Of Crypto". Tactics players bounce off "(HOCAI)".
2. Reply under the weekly Turn Based Thursday host post on Thursdays with the real preview_game.webp and one sentence from the calendar (for example the queue line). No hashtag needed; reply to the host directly. This is the best reach channel for small tactics devs in the sample. The first calendar post (Thu 15 Oct) is the queue post, so it lines up.
3. Use the comparison hook honestly in replies and occasional posts, e.g. "If you read the HoMM battle queue before every move, this board punishes you for not doing it." It fits the "argue about a move" audience.
4. Turn "what would you do" boards into a two-day format: post a real board state, then reply with the answer the next day. This avoids the failing pattern of a daily puzzle spam with an emoji CTA.
5. One Astra post, only if it's factual: who built what with GPT Astra, and the line that A19 is a bounded search, not an LLM. Ask Stepan exactly how Astra is used before drafting. Don't chase the AI-builder crowd beyond that, because they aren't the target player.
