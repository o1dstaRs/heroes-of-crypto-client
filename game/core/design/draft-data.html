Art naming

Portraits resolve through game/core/src/generated/image_imports.ts: creature large = <name>_512, board chip = _128 for 1×1 units and _256 for 2×2, ability = <ability>_256, artifact = artifact_t1_<slug>_256. Names are lowercased with spaces turned into underscores. A missing texture fails silently — it renders as nothing.
